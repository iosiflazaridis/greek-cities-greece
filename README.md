# Greek Cities in Present-Day Greece

An interactive map, statistics and the full list of the 739 cities whose site lies in present-day Greece
and that were Greek at some time between the Mycenaeans and 1821, each with what its date rests on.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/greek-cities-greece/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the 20 cards in full size; `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with a summary, the disputed dates, and the places left out with reasons)

Dates are the first Greek evidence: a Mycenaean palace or Linear B tablets, excavated Early Iron Age settlement, a recorded foundation,
or the first mention (Homer, Herodotus, the tribute lists, coins, a bishop, an Ottoman register). 568 of the 739 rest on a first mention and are lower bounds.
Each city that was Greek by 31 BC is also placed in its ancient dialect area: Attic, Ionic, Aeolic, Arcadian, Doric, or Northwest Greek (Macedonian, Epirote and the rest); Rome's foundations and later towns are given the Greek of their own time (Koine, medieval Greek, early modern Greek).
For 1460–1821 a town counts: a see, the seat of an Ottoman kadi or a Venetian governor, or a community of note (Hydra, Kozani, Ampelakia).

Sources: the Inventory of Archaic and Classical Poleis (Hansen & Nielsen 2004), Homer's Catalogue of Ships, the Athenian tribute lists,
Cohen's *Hellenistic Settlements*, Hierocles' *Synecdemus*, the Notitiae and the council lists, the Barrington Atlas and the Pleiades gazetteer;
every record keeps its own sources and notes. Map: Natural Earth.
